# 🔄 Photos Not Showing? Force Refresh!

## The images are configured correctly, but your browser is caching old images.

### Solution: Hard Refresh Your Browser

**Chrome / Edge:**

- Press: `Ctrl + Shift + R`
- Or: `Ctrl + F5`

**Firefox:**

- Press: `Ctrl + Shift + R`
- Or: `Ctrl + F5`

### Alternative: Clear Browser Cache

1. Open browser
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
3. Select "Cached images and files"
4. Click "Clear data"
5. Refresh the page
